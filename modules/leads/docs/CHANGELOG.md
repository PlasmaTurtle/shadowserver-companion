## 0.12.0

- CVE and end-of-life queues filter the active findings before pagination. A mixed lead appears in both views and shares one review decision. Starting, deferring or ignoring a lead advances within the selected queue.
- Lead details offer separate reported-host and root-domain links. The preview shows the time and destination of the capture selected for the lead’s observations and opens the saved original at full resolution. Opening the lead reuses that capture. [Lead review and evidence](README.md#boundary)
- The dossier shows the canonical entity’s completed-notification count and last delivery date, with access to the case history. [Lead-to-notification verification](../../../docs/history/validation/intake-review-2026-11-22.md#workflow-and-upgrade-evidence)
